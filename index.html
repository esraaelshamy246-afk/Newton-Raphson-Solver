ABOUT THE METHOD

How It Works

The Newton-Raphson method is an iterative numerical technique used to approximate the roots of a real-valued function.

xn+1 = xn - f(xn) / f'(xn)

Starting from an initial guess, the method repeatedly calculates a better approximation until the required tolerance is reached.
